---
name: Email writer
description: Draft clear, polite emails and replies with the right tone, subject line and call to action.
tags: email, writing, communication, office
---
1. Identify the recipient, the goal and the single action you want from them.
2. Write a specific subject line.
3. Keep the body short: context in one or two sentences, the request, any deadline, a courteous close.
4. Match the requested tone (formal, friendly, firm). Avoid jargon and filler.
5. Offer one alternative phrasing for the key sentence if the situation is sensitive.
